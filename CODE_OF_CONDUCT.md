# GoOmni community code of conduct

GoOmni's community should be a useful, respectful place to collaborate. This
applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces maintained
for the project.

## Expected behavior

Treat people with respect, explain technical disagreements with evidence, and
accept corrections. Give constructive feedback and credit contributors. Welcome
people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
Keep shared examples free of private or identifying data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, threats,
impersonation, and publishing someone else's private information are not welcome.
Neither are repeated disruption, spam, or retaliation against someone who reports
a concern. Criticism of a proposal is welcome; abuse of its author is not.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to [the maintainer](mailto:mostafa.a.aut@gmail.com),
using “GoOmni community” in the subject. Include only the context necessary to
understand the incident. Do not post a report containing private information in a
public issue.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, limit disclosure to people needed to
resolve them, and consider context and severity. Responses may include a request
to change behavior, removal of content, a temporary restriction, or a permanent
ban. Maintainers may also remove irrelevant or harmful contributions. A person
subject to an enforcement decision may request a review through the same private
contact channel.

This policy does not promise absolute confidentiality or override legal duties.
All participants, including maintainers, are expected to follow it.
